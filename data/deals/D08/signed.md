# Master Services Agreement

This agreement is made between Lumen Data Ltd. ("Provider") and Ironwood Energy ("Customer").

Effective date: 2026-07-22
Order value: USD 138,000

## Payment Terms

Customer will pay each invoice within 30 days of receipt of the invoice. Overdue amounts accrue interest at 1% per month.

## Price Discount

Provider will apply a volume discount of 8% to the list price for all committed seats.

## Liability Cap

The total liability of each party under this Agreement will not exceed 2x the fees paid by Customer in the 12 months preceding the claim.

## Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for a material breach that remains uncured 30 days after written notice. Neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience.

## Service Levels

Provider will maintain monthly uptime of 99.9%. No service credits are payable for downtime.

## Data Processing

Provider will process personal data only on documented instructions from Customer and will sign the data processing addendum attached as Exhibit B.

## Confidentiality

Each party will protect the other party's confidential information as confidential for 3 years after disclosure.

## Governing Law

This Agreement will be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

## Warranty

Provider warrants that the services will conform to the documentation and will correct any non-conformity within 90 days of notice.
