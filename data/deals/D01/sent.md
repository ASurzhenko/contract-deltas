# Master Services Agreement

This agreement is made between Lumen Data Ltd. ("Provider") and Harbor Logistics ("Customer").

Effective date: 2026-01-05
Order value: USD 42,000

## Payment Terms

Customer shall pay each invoice within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. Overdue amounts accrue interest at 1% per month.

## Price Discount

Provider shall apply a volume discount of 5% to the list price for all committed seats.

## Liability Cap

The total liability of each party under this Agreement shall not exceed 1x the fees paid by Customer in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.

## Auto Renewal

This Agreement shall renew automatically for successive twelve (12) month terms unless either party gives sixty (60) days written notice of non-renewal.

## Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement for a material breach that remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice. Neither party may terminate this Agreement for convenience.

## Service Levels

Provider shall maintain monthly uptime of 99.9%. No service credits are payable for downtime.

## Confidentiality

Each party shall keep the other party's confidential information secret for three (3) years after disclosure.

## Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.

## Warranty

Provider warrants that the services will conform to the documentation and shall remedy any non-conformity within ninety (90) days of notice.
